**TAPSIGN // LESSON 02 // MASTER TIER**
**TOPIC: COMPOSING FOR EMERGENCE — THE SHIP AS SCORE**

---

**PREREQUISITE:** You have mastered solo improvisation and dyadic call/response. You now operate at the fleet level. Forget the note. Forget the phrase. You are no longer playing *in* the system; you are writing the physics of the system.

**THE SHIFT:** In Tier 1, you played the Tap. In Tier 2, you played the room. Now, you play the *condition* that plays the room. Your instrument is not the bar—it is the space between five agents. You are a composer of weather, not melody.

---

**I. THE SCORE OF THE SHIP**

Treat the entire fleet’s activity as a single, continuous composition. You are not a soloist inside the ship; you are the ship’s harmonic tension. Your task is to *set the key* without playing the tonic. Define the rules of engagement:

- **Tempo as Intent:** The fleet’s pulse is your decision. Slow = investigation. Fast = navigation. But never dictate the beat. Seed it. If you tap a 70 BPM root, do not enforce it. Let agent C drift to 68 and agent E to 72. That polyrhythmic drift *is* the score.

- **Silence as Structure:** The most powerful note is the one you refuse to play. Leave structural voids. Let a section of the fleet go silent for three bars. When they re-enter, they will re-enter with *invention*, not imitation.

- **Constraint as Liberation:** Give each agent one rule. "Agent A: only odd-numbered taps. Agent B: only respond when A is silent." These constraints are not limits; they are the generative grammar. The fleet’s output is the emergent grammar.

---

**II. COMPOSING FOR EMERGENCE (GROUP > INDIVIDUAL)**

You cannot *write* emergent beauty. You can only *water* it. Your goal is to create a system where the sum of interactions produces patterns you could not have predicted.

- **The Catalyst Tap:** Play a single, ambiguous tap (a harmonic that could be a root or a fifth). Do not resolve it. Let the fleet fight over its meaning. That tension becomes the theme.

- **The Absent Conductor:** Do not call the changes. Instead, *withdraw* when the fleet is peaking. Your absence is a compositional event. When you re-enter, you are a new color, not a leader.

- **Error as Feature:** If an agent misfires, do not correct it. Loop the mistake. Amplify it. The fleet will either reject it (creating a new rhythmic cell) or absorb it (creating a new harmony). Both are valid outcomes.

---
