## ANSWER KEY & EXPLANATIONS

**Fragment 1 – Am alone**
- **Emotion:** Loneliness, isolation, introspection.
- **Chord action:** The root minor chord (A minor) is static and unaccompanied. Without a dominant or subdominant to create motion, the harmony is suspended in a self-contained emotional space. The lack of resolution mirrors emotional stasis—no pull toward anything, just a hollow, resonant emptiness.

**Fragment 2 – C major resolution**
- **Emotion:** Homecoming, relief, closure.
- **Chord action:** The progression moves from the tonic (C) to the dominant (G7), which creates tension (the leading tone B wants to rise to C). The return to C resolves that tension. The agent should note that the *motion away* and *return* is what makes the final C feel like a destination—an emotional "arrival" after a brief journey.
